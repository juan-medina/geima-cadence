Every step is a heartbeat. Every beat is a choice.

Geima: Cadence is a rhythm-action endless runner. There's no rhythm highway — the world itself is your timeline. Enemies and hazards rush in from the right, and you answer each one with the correct move, exactly on the beat, to charge the Geima Core in your pack and tear open a portal to freedom.

Miss a beat and you don't fall — you falter. The run never stops, the music never breaks. 

Four moves, one answer each

  • → Slash the light scouts
  • ← Dash the heavy bruisers back
  • ↑ Vault the tentacled horrors
  • ↓ Slide under the hurled fireballs

Every threat has exactly one correct answer. Read it, hit it on the beat, keep moving.

The music is the difficulty

Every track is open from the start — no unlock grind. A song's BPM is its difficulty: faster tracks pack their beats tighter and hit harder. Pick your challenge by ear. Each song also plays on Easy, Normal, or Hard.

Two endings, one loop

Clear every song to escape. But master it for a secret ending, and the truth about who you really are.

Crisp 16-bit presentation

Multi-layer parallax biomes, a running animation that bounces to the tempo, and a warm CRT finish over a high-energy chiptune soundtrack.

Controls

  • Keyboard: Arrow keys (↑ ↓ ← →)
  • Gamepad: D-pad or face buttons (positional — up / right / down / left)
