# Geima: Cadence 0.9.0

The first public release — run, slash, dash, vault, and slide to the beat.

## Highlights
- Rhythm-action endless runner: answer every hazard with the matching move, exactly
  on the beat, to charge your escape core.
- Open song selection — every track available from the start; BPM is the difficulty.
- Easy, Normal, and Hard charts per song, with B / A / S rank medals.
- Two goals to chase: escape every song, then master them all.

## Platforms
- **Play in your browser** on itch.io.
- Downloads for **Windows**, **Linux**, and **macOS**.

Thanks for playing!
